# Founder-led growth plan

Apply the shared [operation contract](../references/operation-contract.md). First clarify whether the plan concerns the founder's business or an authorized client, then resolve the matching BAMF space. Gather only needed creator context and available analytics. Ask concise questions when audience, offer, proof, constraints, conversion event, or timeframe are missing; do not invent business facts, customer quotes, metrics, case studies, or traction.

Produce a compact plan with: verified starting evidence and gaps; target audience and offer (confirmed versus assumed); one primary conversion goal; a small set of measurable channel experiments; content themes and next drafts; a site/lead-magnet conversion recommendation; and owner, timeframe, metric, and review point. Use `bamf.get_funnel_mix` or analytics tools to ground content choices where available. Label strategy as a hypothesis and include what would falsify it. A deck or chart is optional only when the host provides a native artifact surface; cite its source range and coverage.

Keep outcomes within actual capability: this connector can prepare content/outreach and managed-site work, but exposes no team/bot administration, universal CRM, arbitrary outbound send, newsletter/community delivery, or general deck generation. Ask before saving drafts/artifacts or changing a site; ask separately before any publish operation. The shared [contract](../references/operation-contract.md) governs receipts and approval.
